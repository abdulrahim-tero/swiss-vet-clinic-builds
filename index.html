Install
  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download APK.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from this browser ("Install unknown apps").
  3. Sign in with any phone number and any 6-digit code.
  4. Payments open a demo payment page — choose "Pay" or "Simulate decline".
التثبيت
  1. افتح هذه الصفحة على الهاتف واضغط تنزيل APK.
  2. افتح الملف الذي تم تنزيله، واسمح بالتثبيت من هذا المتصفح إذا طُلب منك ذلك.
  3. سجّل الدخول بأي رقم هاتف وأي رمز من 6 أرقام.
  4. صفحة الدفع تجريبية — اختر "ادفع" أو "محاكاة الرفض".
What's in this build
  1. Shop → product → cart → checkout → payment → order tracking
  2. Appointments: two-step booking, reschedule, cancel
  3. Health records, visit reports, invoices and invoice payment
  4. Arabic and English (switch in Account; restart to flip the layout)

All names, pets, prices and invoices are sample data. The build is signed with a debug key and cannot be published to Google Play.
